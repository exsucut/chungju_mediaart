## 4. 프롬프트 (아래 본문을 그대로 제출)

```
Use @Image1 as the first frame.
This first frame defines the opening composition and the frame structure: a black bar across the very top and another across the very bottom, and between them a letterboxed picture band holding the scene. In the opening there is no ship and no horizon — the band holds only a cold blue-grey fog bank, rolling cloud masses stacked across the middle, a pale luminous glow burning through from the upper right, and dark water rolling in low swells along the bottom, half swallowed by the fog.

@Image2 defines how the ship must look when it appears later: a modern steel cargo ship seen in three-quarter view from ahead, its raked bow toward the viewer, a boxy superstructure behind it, a tall slender ringed mast with a small dragon-head finial rising almost to the top of the band, and its lower hull buried in fog and broken water. Take from @Image2 only the ship's appearance and how heavily the fog veils it. Do not adopt it as the opening composition.

0 to 3 seconds: no ship anywhere in frame. The camera moves forward through the fog at a normal, steady pace, banks of cloud parting and streaming past the lens. Below, the sea rolls in low swells that travel toward the viewer, crests catching the cold light, foam streaks drawn out along the troughs. At the end of this beat there is still nothing but cloud and water.

3 to 8 seconds: the camera slows almost to a standstill and creeps forward. Out on the right a darker mass thickens in the fog and slowly gathers into the veiled bow of a ship, coming on toward the viewer. The vessel is closest at the end of this beat and is still more than half hidden.

8 to 13 seconds: the camera accelerates hard and sweeps past the ship on its left side, the dark hull passing close to the lens but never sharpening. The ship slides out of frame behind, and the camera carries on forward into the fog bank ahead. The fog closes over the lens and fills the whole picture band. At the end nothing remains but pale luminous cloud, bright and soft, ready to become sky.

THE SHIP IS NEVER FULLY REVEALED. At every moment fog lies between the lens and the vessel. The hull never darkens into a hard black cut-out and never reads as a clean complete silhouette — it stays a soft charcoal mass only a little darker than the fog around it, its edges dissolving into the air, whole sections of it vanishing and returning as banks of fog drift across. The ringed mast is the only part that ever reads sharply, and even that is broken by passing fog. There is never a clear establishing view of the whole ship. The fog never lifts and never thins out.

THE SEA IS NEVER STILL. The ship is under way and cutting through open water the whole time it is visible: a white bow wave peels off the stem in two curling wings, broken white water runs back along the waterline, and a wake trails away behind the stern and spreads into the fog. Most of this is itself half buried in fog — glimpsed, not displayed. The fog drifts and tears continuously. Nothing in this shot is frozen.

THE SHIP MOVES SLOWLY. It holds one steady, unhurried speed from the moment it appears — it never accelerates, never swerves, never stops. All the change in pace belongs to the camera.

The black bar across the top and the black bar across the bottom stay exactly as they are in the first frame for the entire video — the same height, pure black, never moving, never shrinking, and no part of the scene ever crosses into them.

Maintain consistency: one single ship throughout, never duplicated or split; the same hull, superstructure and ringed mast; the same near-monochrome blue-grey grade, brightening only at the very end as the fog takes the frame.

There are no columns, no posts, no pillars and no vertical structures anywhere in the water. There is no land, no building, no other vessel and no person. No text, no lettering, no numerals, no ship name, no markings and no subtitles appear on screen.

The sound is only a low distant fog horn, the muffled wash of the bow wave and the slow roll of the sea, rising as the camera passes and smothered as the fog closes in, with no music, no narration and no dialogue.
```
