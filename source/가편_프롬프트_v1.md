```
【Generation Goal】

【Reference Asset Roles】

【Event Script】
Stage 1 — Start: the camera is inside dense cloud, wisps streaking past the lens, faint dawn light from above. Main event: the camera pushes forward and downward and the cloud tears open in the center. End: through the gap, green land and a silver river are visible but hazy.
Stage 2 — Continuing: the last cloud clears. Main event: the whole walled town from @Image2 is revealed from a high oblique angle, morning mist in the valley, chimney smoke rising. End: the town fills the lower half of the frame, sharp, the fortress wall reading as a continuous line.
Stage 3 — Main event: the camera accelerates into a vertical plunge straight down at the town; roofs and alleys rush up, radial motion blur streaks outward from the frame center, only the center stays sharp. End: the fortress wall crosses the lower frame diagonally and the nearest roofs are close to the lens.

【Maintain Consistency】
The camera never cuts; it is one continuous move from cloud to plunge. The town's layout, wall line and roof color stay as in @Image2. Dawn-to-morning light comes from the upper right throughout. Live-action cinematic realism throughout: ARRI Alexa anamorphic look, fine film grain, subtle halation, projection-safe contrast with true deep blacks and no milky haze. Not a 3D render. No text, lettering, signage, plaques or banners with writing anywhere in the frame. No modern objects.
```

**한 번에 뽑기 위한 장치**: 단계 3의 "radial motion blur, only the center sharp"가 없으면 낙하가 줌으로 대체된다. "never cuts"를 두 번 쓴 이유는 멀티샷 3단계가 컷으로 분리되는 걸 막기 위해서다.
